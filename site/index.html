02 / LESS THINKING. MORE PLAYING.

You bring the feeling.
We’ll hold the chord.

01

Pick your harmony.

Eleven black keys, eleven chord slots. Choose a style, change a voicing, or follow the next-chord hints. Green is a smooth move. Amber adds colour. Red takes a detour.

02

Make it your own.

Brush the white keys for direct string attacks. Hold a chord in Strum or Arp mode for a repeating pattern. Dial in the pace, sweep, gate and swing.

03

Let it ring.

FREE gate lets notes decay naturally. Lock a chord with ENV, explore a new tuning, and keep the voicing you love on each slot. PLAY brings everything to a gentle stop.

GUIT / CHORDI / BETA

Small machine.
Real strings attached.

The quiet beta has been played on an actual FM-1, with controls and audio confirmed by its maker. We’re still checking sustained load, every instrument and restoration before a public firmware release.

Browser saves persist in your browser. Device SAVE currently lasts for the powered session. Standard EADGBE is the default; alternative tunings are independent of your instrument and chord style.
